How the calculator decides

Your run stops at the zone where your damage can no longer keep up with monster HP. Every ancient here multiplies either your damage or your gold, and gold becomes damage by buying hero levels.

Each 25 hero levels multiply damage by 4 and cost 5.43× more gold, so 1% more gold is worth about 0.85% more damage while your best hero is below level 8000, and 0.82% once it's past. With heroes up to Madzi, a new hero takes over around zones 5,800, 13,400, 27,400, 48,900 and 89,900, and each one passes level 8000 a few thousand zones later. Your highest zone decides which value is used. It gives each soul to the ancient where it adds the most damage, counting gold at that rate.

At high levels this lands on fixed ratios: Morvain and Orelian equal to Wrathgar, the gold ancients about 0.9× Wrathgar, Rumbleheart about Wrathgar0.8, and Mortessa about Wrathgar².

99% of your souls go to the damage and gold ancients. The other 1% is split evenly between Kagevar, Animus and Questra. If you own none of those three, everything goes to damage and gold.

The zone estimate compares the damage gain with how fast monster HP and gold grow per zone at your highest zone. Treat it as a rough guide.
